01Importالاستيراد

Import into Egypt, without the waiting.

We prepare the file before your goods sail, settle duties and clear the cargo at port, then deliver it to your warehouse.

What we handle

  1. IMP.1Pre-shipment checkWe review the invoice, packing list and HS codes with you before loading, so nothing is held on arrival.
  2. IMP.2Duties & taxesA clear calculation of customs duties and VAT on the declared value, with no surprises at release.
  3. IMP.3Port releaseDeclaration filing, inspection attendance and release from the terminal.
  4. IMP.4DeliveryOur trucks take the cargo from the port to your door.

Documents we prepare with you

  • D-IMP-1ACID number (Advance Cargo Information)
  • D-IMP-2Commercial invoice & packing list
  • D-IMP-3Bill of lading / air waybill
  • D-IMP-4Certificate of origin
  • D-IMP-5Import approvals & permits, where required
02Exportالتصدير

Egyptian goods, out to the world.

Export declarations, certificates and bookings handled from your factory gate to the vessel.

What we handle

  1. EXP.1Export declarationWe file the export declaration and follow it through customs until the goods are cleared to load.
  2. EXP.2CertificatesCertificate of origin and the other certificates your buyer’s country asks for.
  3. EXP.3Booking & loadingSpace with the shipping line, empty container pick-up and loading at your site.
  4. EXP.4Port handlingGate-in, inspection and loading on the vessel, with the bill of lading sent to you.

Documents we prepare with you

  • D-EXP-1Commercial invoice & packing list
  • D-EXP-2Export declaration
  • D-EXP-3Certificate of origin
  • D-EXP-4Bill of lading
  • D-EXP-5Product certificates, where the buyer requires them

04Custom Servicesالخدمات الجمركية

Customs, handled.

Clearance with Egyptian Customs: classification, declarations and inspection, handled by people who do it every day.

What we handle

  1. CUS.1Tariff classificationThe right HS code for your goods, so the duty is right the first time.
  2. CUS.2Customs declarationWe prepare and file the declaration and answer the customs officers’ questions.
  3. CUS.3InspectionWe attend the inspection with your cargo and coordinate any samples or checks.
  4. CUS.4Release & follow-upIf a shipment is held, we find out why and resolve it.

What we need from you

  • D-CUS-1Commercial invoice
  • D-CUS-2Packing list
  • D-CUS-3Bill of lading / air waybill
  • D-CUS-4Certificate of origin
  • D-CUS-5Company tax card & commercial register
